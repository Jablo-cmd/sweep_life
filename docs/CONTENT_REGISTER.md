## Content handling rules
- Preserve meaning, school values, Christian identity, educational philosophy and distinctive expressions.
- Correct spelling and grammar without rewriting the school's message.
- Confirm factual details with an authorised representative before publication.
- Do not publish invented fees, staff names, school results, testimonials, claims or admissions guarantees.
- Obtain permission for image reuse and verify consent for identifiable learners.
- Replace typographic brand placeholder only when approved logo files are supplied.
- Do not publish private portal data or learner information in the public site.
